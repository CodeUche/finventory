Executive Summary

Audity is a full-stack, multi-tenant business management and accounting platform designed primarily for small and medium enterprises (SMEs) operating in Africa, particularly Nigeria. It combines inventory management, sales invoicing, purchase orders, accounts payable/receivable, payroll processing, tax calculations, and comprehensive financial reporting into a single unified platform.

The platform ships as a native Windows desktop application (built with Tauri + React) as well as a browser-accessible web application backed by a Django REST Framework API. It is architected around strict tenant isolation — each business (organisation) operates in a completely isolated data environment.

Core Value Proposition: Replace the fragmented tools used by African SMEs (spreadsheets, WhatsApp, manual ledgers) with one fully integrated platform that handles everything from point-of-sale to tax filing — while working offline-first via the desktop app.

Problem Statement

Most African SMEs rely on a combination of Excel spreadsheets, paper records, and disconnected mobile apps to manage their finances and operations. This leads to:

  • No real-time visibility into cash flow, inventory levels, or profitability
  • Manual errors in invoicing, stock tracking, and payroll calculations
  • Non-compliance with FIRS tax requirements due to lack of automated tax computation
  • Inability to generate professional financial statements for banking or investor relations
  • No audit trail or role-based access control for team members

Solution

Audity provides a single, affordable SaaS platform that automates all core business processes, posts accurate double-entry journal entries automatically, generates tax-compliant reports, and scales from a one-person shop to a multi-branch enterprise with complex team permissions.

Core Feature Modules

🧾

Sales & Invoicing

Create professional invoices (4 PDF templates), proforma invoices, manage credit sales, record payments, process returns, and send via email or WhatsApp. Recurring invoice automation with Celery beat scheduling.

📦

Inventory Management

Multi-location stock tracking, batch/lot management with expiry notifications, product variants (physical/service/digital), reorder alerts, stock adjustments, and COGS auto-calculation.

🛒

Purchases & Bills

Purchase orders with receipt scanning, accounts payable with folder organisation, bill approval workflows, multi-line tax configuration, vendor management, and payable aging reports.

👥

Customer Management

💹

Accounting & GL

Full double-entry general ledger, automated journal posting for all transactions, Chart of Accounts (auto-seeded), bank reconciliation, balance sheet, P&L, and trial balance.

👷

Employee management, salary structures, automated PAYE (Nigeria PIT) calculation, pension, employee loans, penalties, payslip generation (PDF), and FIRS TaxProMax integration link.

📊

P&L statement, balance sheet, cash flow, VAT returns (downloadable PDF), AR/AP aging, sales analytics by period, top customers, top products, and expense breakdown charts.

💰

Budgeting

Period budgets (daily/weekly/monthly/quarterly/annual), budget line items with quantity × unit price auto-calc, actuals vs. budget variance analysis, and budget activation/approval workflow.

📑

Quotes & Estimates

Draft, send, and convert quotes to invoices. Auto-expiry detection with 5-minute polling, PDF export with all 4 invoice templates, email & WhatsApp sharing, and conversion rate tracking.

💳

Credits Module

Customer credit ledger with full debit/credit history, outstanding balance tracking, GL integration (AR account), payment recording with automatic journal entries, and credit limit enforcement.

🏦

Bank Reconciliation

Create reconciliation sessions, match journal lines to bank statements, toggle cleared status, and reconcile when the difference is within tolerance (±₦0.01).

🔔

Notifications

Real-time notification bell with low-stock alerts, expiring/expired batch warnings, overdue invoice alerts, and 30-second polling for critical business events.

💲

Tax Engine

Nigeria Personal Income Tax (progressive brackets), corporate tax, VAT management with tax classes per product, VAT return generation, and tax configuration per organisation.

⚙️

Settings & Customisation

Organisation branding (logo, brand colour, company stamp), invoice templates (Classic/Modern/Minimal/Professional), font customisation, SMTP email config, team management, and subscription billing.

Recurring Invoice Automation

Recurring invoices are a first-class feature. Business owners create templates (daily/weekly/monthly/quarterly/annual) with configurable intervals. A Celery beat task runs daily to auto-generate invoices, advance the next-run date, log successes/failures, and deactivate templates that have reached their end date or max occurrences.

Document & Folder Organisation

Both invoices and bills support folder-based organisation with nested parent/child folders. The system automatically creates yearly archive folders (e.g. "2025 Archive") on January 1st via a scheduled task, moving all prior-year documents into the archive.

User Roles & Permissions

Audity implements a two-layer permission system: a role hierarchy (numeric levels) for broad access control, and a per-module granular permission matrix for fine-grained sub-account access.

Role Hierarchy

Role Level Description Max Members
Superuser Platform Audity platform administrator. Full access to all organisations, audit logs, and billing management. N/A
Owner 100 Business owner. Full access to all features and settings within their organisation. Can invite members, manage subscriptions. 1
Admin 80 Trusted administrator. Full operational access. Cannot modify subscription plan or owner settings. Plan-limited
Manager 60 Department manager. Can approve budgets, manage team operations. Cannot modify org settings. Plan-limited
Accountant 40 Financial staff. Full accounting and reporting access. Limited inventory & sales access. Plan-limited
Staff 20 Operational staff. View and basic write access per assigned modules. Plan-limited
Viewer 10 Read-only access to assigned modules. Cannot create or modify records. Plan-limited

Module-Level Permission Matrix

Each non-owner member can be assigned per-module access levels independently. The 14 modules with configurable access are:

dashboard sales purchases inventory customers accounting payroll expenses reports budgets credits quotes tax settings

Each module can be set to: none (hidden), view (read-only), write (create/read), or edit (full CRUD). Owners, admins, and platform superusers always receive full access regardless of module settings.

Subscription-Aware Team Limits

The maximum number of active non-owner members is enforced from the organisation's subscription plan (plan.features.max_users). Invitations beyond this limit are rejected with a 403 error. The SubscriptionActive permission class blocks all write operations when the subscription has expired.

Technical Architecture

System Overview

Audity follows a clean separation between a Python/Django backend API and a React frontend. On desktop, the Tauri shell wraps the frontend in a WebView2 container and an HTTP proxy plugin routes API requests through Rust to bypass WebView2 CORS/SSL restrictions.

Tauri HTTP Plugin Pattern: All API calls in the Tauri desktop app route through tauriFetch() (Rust HTTP client) instead of the browser's native fetch(). This bypasses WebView2's certificate and CORS limitations for local/LAN API connections.

Backend Stack

Django 5.1 Django REST Framework SimpleJWT PostgreSQL Celery + Redis django-storages (S3/R2) Gunicorn

Frontend Stack

React 18 TypeScript Vite Tailwind CSS Zustand Axios React Router v6 Recharts jsPDF + AutoTable

Desktop (Tauri)

Tauri v2 Rust tauri-plugin-http tauri-plugin-dialog tauri-plugin-fs WebView2 (Windows)

Infrastructure

Development

  • Django runserver with auto-reload
  • Vite HMR for frontend development
  • SQLite for local testing (pytest)
  • GitHub Actions CI/CD pipeline

Production

  • PostgreSQL (Supabase / Railway)
  • Media storage: S3/Cloudflare R2
  • Celery workers + beat scheduler
  • Gunicorn behind Nginx/Caddy

CI/CD Pipeline

GitHub Actions runs on every push: (1) backend — flake8 lint, pytest with coverage, security audit; (2) frontend — TypeScript type-check, Vite production build; (3) Tauri — cross-platform bundle (generates .msi + .exe installers for Windows).

Authentication & Session Management

Email-based JWT authentication (no username). Access tokens (short-lived) and refresh tokens (long-lived) stored in Zustand persisted state (localStorage). The Axios interceptor auto-refreshes expired access tokens, injects the Authorization header, and attaches the X-Organisation-ID header for tenant resolution.

Data Model Overview

Base Model Hierarchy

Base ModelAddsUsed By
TimeStampedModel UUID PK, created_at, updated_at All models
SoftDeleteModel is_deleted, deleted_at, soft-delete manager All deletable entities
TenantAwareModel organisation FK (tenant isolation) All business data models

Key Domain Entities

  • Organisation — Tenant root. Holds branding, bank details, invoice template preferences, subscription plan.
  • User / Membership — Email-based users attached to one or more organisations via Membership (role-based).
  • Product — Inventory item (physical/service/digital). Has SKU, cost/selling prices, tax class, reorder level.
  • StockItem — Per-product-per-warehouse stock record. Created on first movement. Tracks quantity_on_hand, quantity_reserved.
  • Invoice / SaleItem / SalePayment — Complete sale lifecycle. Auto-posts to GL on creation.
  • Bill / BillPayment — Accounts payable. Auto-posts AP and expense journal entries.
  • JournalEntry / JournalLine — General ledger. Always balanced (debits = credits per entry).
  • Employee / PayrollRun / Payslip — Complete payroll cycle with PAYE bracket engine.
  • RecurringInvoice — Invoice generation template with schedule, items, and run history log.
  • CreditTransaction — Double-entry credit ledger (DEBIT = customer owes, CREDIT = payment received).
  • Budget / BudgetLine — Budgeting with actuals variance computed from GL.
  • ModulePermission — Per-membership, per-module access level (none/view/write/edit).

Tenant Isolation Pattern

Every data model inherits TenantAwareModel. The Django middleware resolves the tenant from the X-Organisation-ID request header and attaches it to request.organisation. All viewsets that extend TenantFilterMixin automatically scope every queryset to the current organisation — it is architecturally impossible to access another tenant's data through normal API routes.

Security Requirements

Authentication

  • Email + password with strong password validation
  • JWT access + refresh token pair (SimpleJWT)
  • Token rotation on refresh
  • Email verification required before login
  • Orphaned account cleanup on re-registration

Authorisation

  • Role-based access control (7 levels)
  • Per-module granular permissions (14 modules)
  • All write operations blocked on expired subscriptions
  • Tenant isolation enforced at ORM layer
  • Superuser-only platform admin routes

Data Protection

  • Soft deletes — no hard data deletion by default
  • All UUIDs (no enumerable integer IDs)
  • Sensitive fields (passwords, SMTP credentials) never returned in API responses
  • Audit log for all admin actions

API Security

  • CORS configured for known frontend origins only
  • Input validation on all serializers (max_length, min_value)
  • Max 200 line items per invoice (payload size guard)
  • SMTP SSL certificates verified (configurable for legacy servers)
  • SQL injection impossible (Django ORM parameterised queries)

Compliance Note: Audity is architected to support NDPR (Nigeria Data Protection Regulation) compliance — all personal data is tenant-isolated, deletion is tracked with soft-delete timestamps, and data exports are role-gated. Full NDPR compliance audit is planned before v1 public launch.

Non-Functional Requirements

Performance

API responses <200ms for list endpoints with up to 10,000 records per tenant. PDF generation <3s for invoices with 50+ line items. Dashboard loads <1s (parallel API calls).

Availability

99.5% uptime SLA for the cloud backend. Desktop app works fully offline (all data cached locally via Tauri HTTP plugin). Celery beat tasks are idempotent and safe to re-run.

Scalability

Tenant-isolated architecture allows horizontal scaling. Database queries are indexed on organisation, is_deleted, and date fields. Pagination on all list endpoints (default 50 per page).

Reliability

Celery tasks have max_retries=3. Double-entry accounting enforces balanced journals at the ORM level. Invoice number collisions are prevented by org-specific hex prefix.

Usability

All monetary inputs use formatted text fields with comma-separators. Dates use DD/MM/YYYY (Africa-standard). Dark/light mode toggle. Sidebar navigation with collapsible groups.

Maintainability

Backend: Django migrations for all schema changes. Frontend: TypeScript strict mode. CI enforces lint, tests, and type-check on every commit. Coverage tracked via pytest-cov.

Localisation

Currency symbol and formatting per organisation's currency setting. Date format: DD/MM/YYYY globally. Initial support: NGN. Multi-currency planned for v2.

Accessibility

Keyboard-navigable forms. Sufficient colour contrast in both dark and light themes. Screen reader-friendly semantic HTML. Print styles for all PDF-generating pages.

Subscription & Billing

Audity uses a plan-based subscription model managed through the subscriptions app. All plan limits and feature flags are stored in a JSON features field, making them configurable without code changes.

Plan Feature Flags

Feature FlagTypeDescription
max_productsIntegerMax products in inventory (999999 = unlimited)
max_usersIntegerMax active non-owner team members
max_warehousesIntegerMax locations/branches (999999 = unlimited)
multi_warehouseBooleanCan use more than one location
advanced_reportsBooleanAccess to balance sheet, P&L, cash flow
api_accessBooleanREST API access for external integrations
recurring_invoicesBooleanRecurring invoice automation
payrollBooleanFull payroll module access

Subscription Enforcement

The SubscriptionActive permission class is applied globally to all write operations. Organisations with an expired subscription receive a 403 Forbidden response with a clear upgrade prompt. Read operations are unaffected, ensuring data remains accessible.

Billing Page

The in-app billing page displays the current plan, usage metrics (products used vs. limit, team members, locations), all available plan features, and upgrade/downgrade options. Billing is managed externally (Paystack or manual for early-stage customers).

Constraints & Assumptions

Technical Constraints

  • Desktop app is currently Windows-only (WebView2 dependency). macOS and Linux planned.
  • PDF generation is client-side (jsPDF). Complex layouts may differ slightly across browsers.
  • Celery requires a running Redis instance — not available on serverless deployments.
  • SQLite is supported for development and testing only; PostgreSQL required for production.
  • File uploads are capped by Nginx client_max_body_size (default 10MB); configurable.

Business Constraints

  • Initial market focus is Nigeria; multi-country tax engines require separate localisation work.
  • No mobile app in v1 — responsive web is the mobile strategy.
  • Manual billing management in early stage; self-serve billing requires Paystack integration.
  • Automated database backups must be configured at the hosting provider level (Railway, Supabase, etc.).

Assumptions

  • Users have at least one organisation created before accessing any module.
  • A StockItem record is only created when the first inventory movement is recorded for a product-location pair.
  • Double-entry accounting is always enforced: every financial transaction posts balanced journal entries automatically.
  • The platform assumes Nigerian Naira (NGN) as the default currency; currency is configurable per organisation.
  • Invoice numbers are globally unique and collision-free via org-UUID-derived prefix (INV-XXXX-000001).
