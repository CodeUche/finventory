Installing — about a minute

1

Click Download for Windows. If your browser asks, choose Keep to finish the download.

2

Open Audity-Setup.exe and follow the prompts. Audity launches automatically when it's done.

3

Sign in with the credentials we sent you — and you're in.

If Windows shows a “Windows protected your PC” screen: that's expected for new apps in beta — Audity is safe. Click More info, then Run anyway.
